Frequently Asked Questions

What type of resin works best with the Photon Mono 4 Ultra?

The printer works excellently with both standard resins and high-speed resins 2.0. For maximum speed, use high-speed resin at 120mm/h, while standard resin achieves 80mm/h speeds. Anycubic's own resins are optimized for this printer, but most third-party 405nm UV resins will work well.

How difficult is the setup process for beginners?

The Photon Mono 4 Ultra comes pre-assembled and factory calibrated, making setup relatively straightforward. You'll need to level the build plate (4-point manual leveling) and install the slicing software. First-time resin printer users should allow 1-2 hours for setup and familiarization with resin handling safety procedures.

What maintenance does this printer require?

Regular maintenance includes cleaning the VAT after failed prints, replacing the FEP film when scratched or cloudy, and occasionally lubricating the Z-axis screw. The LCD screen has a lifespan of approximately 2,000 printing hours before needing replacement. The intelligent lifespan management system helps track component usage.

Can I use this printer for commercial purposes?

Absolutely. The print quality is professional-grade suitable for commercial miniature production, jewelry making, prototyping, and small-scale manufacturing. Many small businesses use this and similar Anycubic printers for commercial production due to their reliability and output quality.

How does the WiFi connectivity work?

The WiFi capability allows wireless printing through the Anycubic App. You can send print files directly from your computer or mobile device, monitor print progress remotely, and access the Makeronline model library. The connection is typically stable for most home networks.
