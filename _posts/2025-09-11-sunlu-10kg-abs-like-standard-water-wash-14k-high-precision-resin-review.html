Practical Usage Tips and Techniques

Having worked with all four resin types extensively, I've developed some practical tips that will help you get the best results from this SUNLU bundle.

Storage and Handling

Despite the one-year shelf life, resin performance gradually decreases over time. Store bottles in a cool, dark place and use them in rotation rather than working through one type completely before moving to the next. Always shake bottles thoroughly before use—sedimentation can affect printing consistency.

Printing Parameters

While all resins work at 405nm, they require slightly different exposure settings. Start with SUNLU's recommended settings for your printer model, then fine-tune based on results. The standard and water-wash resins typically need less exposure time than the ABS-Like and high-precision formulations.

Post-Processing Differences

The water-wash resin truly simplifies cleaning—use warm water and gentle agitation rather than alcohol. For the other resins, ensure proper alcohol cleaning and complete curing. The ABS-Like resin responds well to sanding and drilling, while the high-precision resin benefits from gentle handling to preserve fine details.
