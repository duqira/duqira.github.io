Frequently Asked Questions

What materials can I print with the Kobra 3 Combo?

The main printer supports PLA, PETG, and TPU (single-color only). The ACE PRO system expands this to include ABS, ASA, PET, PA, PC, PP, and HIPS for multi-color printing.

Does it work with third-party slicers?

Yes, the Kobra 3 works with Cura, OrcaSlicer, and other third-party software, but for the best multi-color experience, ANYCUBIC recommends their own slicer which is now open source.

How difficult is it to set up multi-color printing?

Remarkably easy thanks to the integrated system. The ACE PRO handles filament loading, color identification, and switching automatically—you just select your colors in the slicer.

What's the actual print quality at 600mm/s?

Surprisingly good due to the vibration compensation system. For most practical applications, printing at 300-400mm/s provides excellent quality while still being incredibly fast.

Can I upgrade to more than 4 colors?

The AE Version is limited to 4 colors simultaneously. For more colors, you would need to look at higher-end systems like the Prusa XL with multiple toolheads.

How reliable is the automatic filament switching?

Extremely reliable in testing. The system detects when a spool is nearly empty and automatically switches to another spool of the same material and color.

Is the Wi-Fi connection stable for remote monitoring?

Yes, the connection remains stable, and you can also use LAN mode for even more reliable local network control without internet dependency.
