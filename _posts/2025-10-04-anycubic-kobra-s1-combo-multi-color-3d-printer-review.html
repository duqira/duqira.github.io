Revolutionary CoreXY Architecture: Stability Meets Speed

The heart of the Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo's exceptional performance lies in its CoreXY construction—a design typically found in professional-grade printers costing twice as much. Unlike traditional Cartesian systems where the print bed moves in the Y-axis, CoreXY keeps the bed stationary while dual motors move the print head in both X and Y directions. This eliminates the inertia and vibration issues that plague faster printers, allowing the Kobra S1 to achieve its remarkable 600mm/s maximum speed without sacrificing precision.

In practical terms, this means you can print a complete Benchy calibration model in just 15 minutes at recommended speeds of 300mm/s—about 12 times faster than standard FDM printers. For prototype developers, this translates to overnight print jobs becoming afternoon tasks. I tested this with a complex mechanical part that normally took 8 hours on my previous printer; the Kobra S1 completed it in 45 minutes with comparable quality. The dual motor drive system ensures that even at these extreme speeds, the extruder maintains responsive, precise movements that result in clean, accurate prints.

The stability benefits extend beyond just speed. The stationary print bed means your models aren't being shaken during printing, which is particularly crucial for tall, thin prints that would normally wobble and fail. Educational institutions will appreciate this feature when printing architectural models or detailed figurines that require absolute stability throughout the printing process.

Multi-Color Printing: Beyond Single-Filament Limitations

The integrated ACE PRO system transforms the Kobra S1 from a monochrome workhorse into a vibrant color creation tool. With four dedicated filament slots, the printer can handle basic four-color printing right out of the box—no additional modifications or expensive upgrades required. But what truly sets this system apart is its scalability: connect two ACE PRO units, and you unlock eight-color printing capabilities that rival professional-grade machines costing thousands more.

During testing, I printed a multi-colored topographic map that would normally require hours of post-printing painting. The Kobra S1 handled the color changes seamlessly, with the ACE PRO's anti-entanglement design preventing the filament tangles that often plague multi-material setups. Each spool sits in its own isolated slot with built-in buffering mechanics that detect and prevent tangling before it becomes a problem. When one filament runs out, the system automatically switches to another spool of the same material and color—a feature that's invaluable for long prints where filament exhaustion would normally mean starting over.

The practical applications are endless: educators can print full-color anatomical models, architects can create multi-material structural demonstrations, and hobbyists can produce game pieces and miniatures that don't require tedious hand-painting. The current promotional pricing makes this multi-color capability accessible to users who previously couldn't justify the expense of dedicated multi-material systems.

Performance Analysis: Real-World Testing Results

After extensive testing across various materials and print types, the Kobra S1 Combo consistently delivered exceptional results that belie its mid-range price point. The 20,000 mm/s² acceleration allows the printer to reach its maximum speeds almost instantly, yet the resonance compensation technology eliminates the ringing artifacts that typically plague fast printers. Using the built-in accelerometer, the printer automatically detects and compensates for resonance frequencies, resulting in smooth surfaces even on complex geometries.

The 1+1+1 cooling system—consisting of a main fan and two auxiliary fans—provides immediate cooling that's crucial for maintaining detail at high speeds. When printing PLA overhangs at 300mm/s, I observed minimal drooping or deformation, something that would challenge printers costing twice as much. The full-metal hotend maintains consistent temperatures even during rapid extrusion, preventing the clogs that often occur when pushing materials quickly through the nozzle.

Where the Kobra S1 truly shines is in its material versatility. The 320°C hotend and 120°C heated bed handled everything from basic PLA to advanced ASA with equal competence. Printing ASA—a material known for its warping tendencies—was surprisingly straightforward thanks to the stable bed temperature and enclosed design. The aerospace-grade ceramic composite throat tube reduces friction while withstanding high temperatures, meaning fewer jams and more consistent extrusion across material types.

One of the most impressive features is the simultaneous drying and printing capability. The ACE PRO system maintains optimal filament humidity during printing, eliminating the need for separate drying boxes that add cost and complexity. For users in humid environments, this feature alone can prevent countless failed prints due to moisture-laden filament.
