Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How should I store this filament when not in use?

A: Like all PLA filaments, store in a sealed container with desiccant to prevent moisture absorption. The 2-year shelf life assumes proper storage conditions.

Q: Can I use this filament with any 3D printer?

A: Yes, it works with any printer that uses 1.75mm filament. However, to utilize the high-speed capabilities, you'll need a printer capable of faster movement speeds.

Q: How does the dual-color effect work?

A: The filament is manufactured with two colors blended in a specific pattern that creates gradient effects when printed. The effect varies based on print orientation and layer height.

Q: What's the best nozzle temperature for this filament?

A: I found 210°C works well for most applications, but refer to the recommended range of 190-220°C and adjust based on your specific printer and needs.

Q: Is this filament suitable for food-safe applications?
