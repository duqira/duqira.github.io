FAQ Section

How should I store unused spools?

Keep spools in their original vacuum-sealed packaging until ready for use. Once opened, store in airtight containers with desiccant packets to maintain optimal dryness.

What printer settings work best?

Recommended settings: nozzle temperature 220-250°C, bed temperature 70-80°C, print speed 40-60mm/s. Start at 235°C nozzle and 75°C bed for optimal results.

Is this filament food-safe?

While PETG is generally considered food-safe, 3D printed items have microscopic layers that can harbor bacteria. We recommend using for decorative items rather than direct food contact.

How does it compare to PLA?

PETG offers superior durability, heat resistance, and flexibility compared to PLA. It's less brittle and better suited for functional parts and outdoor use.

What's the shelf life of unopened spools?

Properly stored in original packaging, the filament should maintain optimal quality for 12-18 months. The vacuum sealing and desiccant provide excellent protection.

Can I use this with any 1.75mm printer?

Yes, it's compatible with most FDM printers using 1.75mm filament. It works particularly well with Creality printers but performs excellently on other brands too.
