A: Water washing is simpler, safer, and more cost-effective. It eliminates flammable chemicals and reduces ongoing material costs. The cleanup process is equally effective for most applications.

Q: Can I use this resin in any LCD printer?

A: Yes, it works with any LCD/LED 3D printer using 405nm wavelength. Always check your printer's compatibility, but it works with most consumer-grade resin printers.

Q: How should I dispose of the wash water?

A: The resin particles will settle out if left standing. Dispose of clear water carefully and cure any residue before disposal. Local regulations may vary.

Q: Does the reduced odor mean no ventilation is needed?

A: While odor is significantly reduced, some ventilation is still recommended. The resin still emits vapors during printing, so adequate airflow is important.

Q: How long does the resin last once opened?
