Advanced Thermal Management System

Heat management is where the Kraken truly distinguishes itself from competitors. This isn't just marketing speak—in practical terms, this means your drivers run cooler, maintain consistent performance, and enjoy extended lifespan.

For users who've experienced thermal throttling or driver shutdown during long prints, the Kraken's cooling solution represents a fundamental improvement. The thermal performance alone justifies the investment for serious printing applications.

Power Delivery and Motor Control

The Kraken's eight onboard TMC2160 drivers represent another significant upgrade over previous generations. Supporting input voltages from 24-60V, these drivers provide exceptional flexibility for various motor types and configurations. The M1-M4 sockets deliver up to 4.7A driving current, while M5-M8 provide up to 3A—this tiered approach allows optimal power distribution based on your specific motor requirements.

In practical terms, this means you can drive high-torque NEMA 17 motors for X and Y axes while allocating appropriate power to extruder and Z motors. The TMC2160 drivers operating in SPI mode provide exceptional step smoothing and noise reduction, resulting in noticeably quieter operation compared to older driver technologies.

Connectivity and Expansion Capabilities

The Kraken's expansion interfaces read like a wishlist for serious 3D printing enthusiasts. Dual JST ports with CAN support allow connection of two EBB SB2240/2209 CAN boards, enabling advanced toolhead configurations like StealthBurner systems. This eliminates cable drag and simplifies wiring while maintaining reliable communication.

Beyond CAN connectivity, the board includes multiple temperature sensor interfaces (5x 100K NTC, 2x MAX31865 for PT100/PT1000), extensive endstop options, RGB lighting support, and dedicated ports for probes, servos, and filament sensors. The inclusion of a 5V/3A power supply for Raspberry Pi or BIGTREETECH Pi products simplifies your system architecture by eliminating separate power supplies.

During my build process, I particularly appreciated the thoughtful port arrangement—everything is logically organized and clearly labeled, making wiring straightforward even for complex multi-toolhead setups. The Type-C interface for PC communication provides modern connectivity without the fragility of Micro-USB ports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Kraken compatible with my existing Voron 2.4?

Yes, the Kraken is specifically designed for Voron 2.4 and Trident printers. It uses standard mounting patterns and includes all necessary connections for these builds.

Can I use this with 24V and 48V systems?

Absolutely. The drivers support 24-60V input, making them compatible with both standard 24V systems and higher-voltage 48V configurations for improved motor performance.

Does it include built-in Wi-Fi or Ethernet?

No, the Kraken relies on external solutions like Raspberry Pi for network connectivity. This keeps the board focused on its core function of motor control and thermal management.

How difficult is the firmware configuration?

Configuration requires intermediate technical skills, but extensive documentation and community support are available. Sample configurations for popular printers make the process manageable.

Can I use TMC2209 drivers instead of the onboard TMC2160?

No, the TMC2160 drivers are onboard and not replaceable. However, their performance exceeds that of TMC2209 drivers, particularly in high-current applications.

Is the Pi power supply sufficient for Raspberry Pi 4?

Yes, the 5V/3A supply provides adequate power for Raspberry Pi 4 models, even under load with connected peripherals.
