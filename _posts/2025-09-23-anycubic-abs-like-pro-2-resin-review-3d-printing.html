FAQ Section

What printers are compatible with this resin?

The Anycubic ABS-Like+ Pro 2 resin works with any LCD or DLP 3D printer that operates within the 365-405nm wavelength range. This includes most consumer and prosumer resin printers from brands like Anycubic, Elegoo, Creality, and others. Always check your printer's specifications to confirm compatibility.

How should I store the 10kg container?

Store the resin in its original container in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. Ensure the lid is tightly sealed to prevent contamination and reduce exposure to oxygen. The resin has a shelf life of one year when properly stored.

What safety precautions are necessary?

Always wear nitrile gloves and safety glasses when handling uncured resin. Work in a well-ventilated area, and consider using a respirator rated for organic vapors if ventilation is limited. Cure all waste resin before disposal according to local regulations.

Can I mix colors with this resin?

While possible, mixing colors may affect the resin's mechanical properties and curing characteristics. For consistent results, it's recommended to use pre-mixed colors rather than attempting custom color blends unless you're experienced with resin modification.

What exposure settings should I use?

Start with Anycubic's recommended exposure settings for your specific printer model, typically available on their website or product documentation. Always conduct exposure calibration tests to optimize settings for your specific printing environment and equipment.

How does this compare to true ABS plastic?

While not identical to injection-molded ABS, this resin closely approximates ABS's mechanical properties while offering the detail resolution of resin printing. It's more brittle than actual ABS but significantly tougher than standard resins, making it suitable for many functional applications.

Is the 10kg package practical for home users?

For frequent printers who use resin regularly, the 10kg package offers significant cost savings. However, occasional users might find smaller quantities more practical despite the higher per-unit cost, unless they have proper storage and anticipate increased usage.
