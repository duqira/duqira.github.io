Frequently Asked Questions

What printers is this filament compatible with?

This filament works with any FDM printer that accepts 1.75mm filament, including popular models from Creality, Prusa, Anycubic, Flashforge, and many others. No special modifications are required.

How long does the color transition take?

The color transition occurs gradually over approximately 20-25 meters of filament, creating smooth gradients rather than abrupt color changes. The exact transition length can vary slightly between spools.

Does it require special printing settings?

Print temperatures of 190-210°C work well, typically about 5-10°C lower than standard PLA to maintain the silk finish. Bed temperature of 50-60°C is recommended for best adhesion.

How should I store this filament?

Like all PLA filaments, store in a dry, cool place with low humidity. Use airtight containers with desiccant for long-term storage to prevent moisture absorption.

Can I pause prints and resume later?

Yes, but be aware that the color transition will continue during the pause. For best results, try to complete prints in single sessions when possible.

Is this filament food-safe?

While PLA is generally considered food-safe, the additives for color and silk effect mean this specific filament should not be used for items that will contact food.

How many projects can I complete with one spool?

A 1kg spool typically allows for 15-20 medium-sized decorative items or 5-8 larger projects, depending on infill density and size.
