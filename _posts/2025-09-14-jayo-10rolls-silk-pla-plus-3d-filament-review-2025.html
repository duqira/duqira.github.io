While generally excellent, the filament does have some limitations to consider. The silk finish, while beautiful, can show layer lines more visibly than matte finishes on certain geometries. For projects requiring specific color consistency across multiple prints, traditional single-color filaments might be more appropriate. However, for most creative applications, this variability adds character rather than detracting from results.
