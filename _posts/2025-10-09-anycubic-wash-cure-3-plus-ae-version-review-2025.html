Revolutionary Curing Technology

Intelligent IPA Conservation System

One of the most costly aspects of resin printing is isopropyl alcohol consumption. Traditional washing methods waste significant amounts of IPA, both through evaporation and contamination. The dual-layer PP material cleaning basket is a brilliantly simple solution to this problem.

The system offers two adjustable height settings (150mm and 250mm), allowing you to match the fluid volume precisely to your print platform size. For smaller prints from 6.6-inch printers, you use less alcohol. For larger 10.6-inch platforms, you have the capacity needed without excessive waste. In my testing, this feature reduced my IPA consumption by approximately 40% compared to my previous setup.

The dual-layer curing platform similarly improves efficiency by allowing multiple models to cure simultaneously. Instead of processing prints individually or in small batches, you can maximize each curing cycle, saving both time and energy. For production environments or enthusiastic hobbyists with high output, these efficiency improvements quickly justify the equipment cost.
