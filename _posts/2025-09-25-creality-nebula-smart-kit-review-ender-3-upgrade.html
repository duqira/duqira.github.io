Advanced Printing Features

Beyond the obvious remote monitoring capabilities, the Nebula Smart Kit introduces several advanced features that improve print reliability and success rates:

Motion Advance Function: This optimizes filament feeding to reduce blobs and oozing, resulting in cleaner prints with better surface quality. In my testing, I noticed particularly improved performance on complex prints with many retractions.

Filament Runout Detection: The system monitors filament usage and will pause printing and notify you when you're running low. No more failed prints due to unexpected filament exhaustion.

Power Loss Recovery: If you experience a power outage, the Nebula Pad stores print progress and can resume exactly where it left off once power is restored. This is invaluable for long prints in areas with unreliable power.

Multi-Language Support: With support for 10 languages including English, German, French, Spanish, Chinese, and Japanese, the system is accessible to users worldwide.
