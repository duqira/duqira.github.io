About the Author

Tech Print Expert

With over 5 years of hands-on experience in the 3D printing industry, I've tested hundreds of 3D printers across all price ranges and technologies. My journey began with a simple desktop FDM printer and has evolved into comprehensive testing of industrial-grade additive manufacturing systems.

Background & Experience

  • 5+ years in 3D printing technology testing and review
  • Extensive experience with FDM, SLA, SLS, and DLP technologies
  • Former 3D printing technician and consultant
  • Regular contributor to 3D printing communities and forums
  • Certified in multiple 3D printing software and hardware platforms

Testing Philosophy

I believe in practical, real-world testing that reflects how people actually use 3D printers. My reviews focus on:

  • Out-of-box experience and setup process
  • Print quality across various materials
  • Reliability and consistency over time
  • Value for money at different price points
  • Community support and manufacturer responsiveness

Areas of Specialization

  • Desktop FDM 3D Printers (Creality, Prusa, Anycubic, etc.)
  • Resin 3D Printers (Elegoo, Anycubic, Phrozen, etc.)
  • 3D Printing Materials Science
  • Slicing Software Optimization
  • Print Quality Troubleshooting
